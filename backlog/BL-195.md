Found closing BL-175 (2026-11-17), live on crunchtools/josui#84. The list read had mergeStateStatus BLOCKED with checks passing. The block came from unresolved threads, which the list read can't see. verdictGlyph (internal/tui/changes.go) finds none of conflicts, failing, changes requested, behind, running, or review required, so it falls through to checksGlyph and shows a green ✓. That is the same glyph as Ready, while the change view strip says ✕ Blocked. D46 specifies this fallback, but it breaks the epic's principle that nothing not ready reads ready.

Decision: a BLOCKED merge state with no list-visible reason shows a dedicated red `⊘`, after review required and before ready in the precedence. A ✓ without green would still read as ready under NO_COLOR, and ○ already means review required. Verify that ⊘ is one cell wide in a PTY under default, NO_COLOR, and high contrast, and fall back to another single-width glyph if it isn't. Update D46, the help legend (Change column), and docs/tui.md Rows.
