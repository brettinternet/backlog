### Why

A workspace TUI ranks items by claim state but drops everything else: no Claim column or detail lease, no expiry refresh, and no renewal. A claim started there lapses after one lease while the person works, and an agent can then take the item, with nothing on screen to show it.

### Shape

- The workspace projection reads each coordinated project's claim list, off the update loop as today, and keeps each item's claim: `mine`, `agent@host`, or `expired`, with its lease in the detail header, the same as a single project.
- The TUI renews every claim it holds through that claim's own project, at half the first remaining lease.
- Claim expiry refreshes the workspace view without waiting for the poll.
- docs/tui.md and design.md describe workspace claims.
