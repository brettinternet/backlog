A tour goes stale as soon as the change gets a new head, so a reviewer returning after the author pushes fixes loses it. Instead of hiding a stale tour, map each step from the tour's ref to the current head through the diff between them, like outdated review comments on a forge: unchanged lines move to their new position, edited or deleted lines are marked outdated. Costs no agent tokens. Mapping runs on the detail worker, never the update loop (D14).

## Notes

BL-163's tours.mode require counts a change as covered only when its stored tour's ref is the current head (engine.ToursPending). When carrying tours forward, extend ToursPending so a carried tour with no outdated steps satisfies require.

## Summary

The TUI Diff now carries a tour for an earlier head forward when ref is an ancestor of the head. changes.ReadMoves diffs ref..head, and the two old sides, from Git objects only. Moves.Range maps each step: unchanged ranges move to their new lines and follow renames; ranges that were edited, deleted, or had lines added inside are outdated and show their ref lines under the file header, where ]t/[t put the cursor. A banner names both commits and the outdated count. Rewritten history stays stale. The mapping runs on the detail worker and is cached by dir, ref, and head.
