The Relations tab lists blockers, blocked items, the parent, children, related items, and duplicates, but none of them can be opened by keyboard or mouse. Reaching one means reading its ID and typing it into go-to (ctrl+p). Make every entry followable, and make the jump reversible. Reuse the Criteria tab's cursor (j/k, click to select) and go-to's way of opening an item's detail without changing the view. Related and duplicate-of IDs render inline on one line today and need per-entry targets.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] In a focused Relations tab, j/k move a cursor over every relation entry, including each related and duplicate-of ID, and enter opens the target's detail without changing the view or the list selection.
- [ ] Clicking an entry selects it and double-clicking opens it, matching list rows.
- [ ] A documented back key returns to the previous item through any number of jumps, ending at the original selection.
- [ ] A missing or unloaded target is marked and says why it can't open; following does no I/O or backlog-size work on Update (D14).
- [ ] docs/tui.md (Detail and Mouse) describes the behavior; tests cover follow, back, and a missing target; task ci passes.
