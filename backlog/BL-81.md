D20 says "bl knows about claims, not about Worklease". The interface holds: `model.Coordinator` is narrow, outcomes are typed, `RecoveryRef` is opaque, and engine, TUI, and MCP tests use fake coordinators. The leaks are around it:

- `worklease.New(cfg)` is constructed behind the same `if cfg.Coordination.Worklease != nil` in three places (`internal/cli/root.go`, `internal/mcp/server.go`, `internal/engine/workspace.go`).
- About ten engine, CLI, MCP, and hooks sites test `cfg.Coordination.Worklease != nil` to mean "coordination is on" (`readiness.go`, `projection.go`, `next.go`, `read.go`, `coordination.go`, `workspace.go`, `write.go`, `hooks/diagnose.go`). A second claim system would have to touch all of them.
- `model.ClaimInfo.Host` is never set by the adapter, so the `agent@host` branch of `ClaimInfo.Label` is dead.

The goal is to keep provider knowledge at construction, configuration, and doctor. Do not add a public coordinator protocol or plugin registry; D18 waits for a second claim system. Leave the WORKLEASE_* caller-context environment reading in `root.go` as design §9 specifies.
