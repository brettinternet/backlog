### Why

The detail pane doesn't show who holds an item or for how long, and the Claim column only appears on wide lists without any time.

### Shape

- With coordination enabled, the detail meta row adds the claim after priority: `mine 12m`, `agent@host 4m`, or `expired`, using the same colors as the Claim column. Under a minute shows `<1m`.
- The remaining time comes from the claim list the TUI already polls; rendering does no I/O and stays current with the 5-second poll.
- docs/tui.md describes it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With coordination enabled, the detail meta row shows mine, the holder agent@host, or expired, with the remaining lease such as 12m or <1m
- [ ] The value comes from the polled claim list without I/O during rendering and updates with the claim poll
- [ ] docs/tui.md describes the detail claim
