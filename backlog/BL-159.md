A tour step can cover a range of lines, not just one. Accept CodeTour's selection ({"start":{"line":N,"character":C},"end":{"line":M,"character":C}}, 1-based lines) on a step, with side applying to the whole range. The callout anchors above the range's first line, and every line in the range gets a gutter mark in the Diff tab, in unified and split views, so the reviewer sees what the narrative covers. Characters are ignored: highlighting is whole lines. Builds on BL-151, which refuses unknown keys, including selection, so a range is never silently cut down to one line.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A step may give selection in place of or alongside line; when both are given, line must equal selection.start.line, and an end before the start, a range over 500 lines, or a non-integer line is refused with the reason, through the shared tour validator
- [ ] The Diff tab marks every line of a step's range in the gutter on the step's side, in unified and split views, and the callout sits above the first line
- [ ] Range lines outside every hunk come in with the same three lines of context as a single out-of-hunk step, and lines the file lacks are noted under the file header
- [ ] ]t and [t put the cursor on the range's first line and keep as much of the range visible as the pane allows
- [ ] docs/schemas/tour.json, bl instructions tours, docs/tui.md, and design.md D43 describe ranges
