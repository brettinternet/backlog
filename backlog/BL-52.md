## Why

Backlog.md 1.52 writes each criterion and definition-of-done entry as `- [ ] #1 text`, and renumbers the `#N` index whenever an entry is added or removed. The importer keeps `#1` as part of the text, and `bl` never renumbers it, so after an edit the text names the wrong number. That is the stale-number problem D28 removed. This repository's imported tasks carried these indexes until they were cleaned by hand.

## What

- During import, strip a leading `#N ` index from task-list entries in Acceptance criteria and Definition of done, alongside the HTML markers, keeping the text and checked state.
- Report the removal as a mapping decision, and make docs/import.md match.
- BL-36 reuses this decoder for reads and writes the index back.
