Relation edits reach bl only at the hourly reconciliation because Linear does not bump the issue updatedAt. The top-level issueRelations connection may support orderBy: updatedAt (and includeArchived), which would let the minute scan page newest relations until it passes the watermark.

Verify against the live API before building: ordering and paging behavior, whether removed relations appear (archived) or vanish, workspace-wide scope and team filtering, and request/complexity cost per empty window. If removals are invisible, the scan can still add new blockers quickly while reconciliation keeps handling removals.
