The Relations tab lists blockers, blocked items, the parent, children, related items, and duplicates, but none of them can be opened by keyboard or mouse. Reaching one means reading its ID and typing it into go-to (ctrl+p). Make every entry followable, and make the jump reversible. Reuse the Criteria tab's cursor (j/k, click to select) and go-to's way of opening an item's detail without changing the view. Related and duplicate-of IDs render inline on one line today and need per-entry targets.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] In a focused Relations tab, j/k move a cursor over every relation entry, including each related and duplicate-of ID, and enter opens the target's detail without changing the view or the list selection.

## Plan

Engine: RelationRows emits duplicate-of and related relations; Projection carries a Catalog that prepares any item's row (inbound relations, readiness) in O(item) from the projector's cached reverse index.

TUI: generalize the Criteria cursor to entries; Relations entries carry targets; enter and double-click follow through the catalog (as go-to opens an item), backspace unwinds a history stack to the list selection; a missing target explains why from source state.
