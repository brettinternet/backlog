Readiness reports an item carrying a ready.excludeLabels label as `blocked`, even when every dependency is satisfied. That conflates a dependency fact (a known unsatisfied blocker) with queue policy, so `is:blocked` and the blocked wording mislead, for example for `human` work.

Map the `excluded-label` reason to `ineligible`, alongside status and container. `blocked` then means only a known unsatisfied blocker. Reason codes are unchanged. Per-actor eligibility (ready for the human, excluded for agents) is out of scope; assignment already covers it.
