Mouse equivalent of < and >: drag a card to another column to change its status. A drop must use the same write and undo path as >. Draft until these are decided: what a drop means when the Board is grouped by something other than status (labels and assignees are multi-valued); how far a drag must travel so a click or trackpad jitter never writes; how the target column and a refused transition (for example a Jira workflow that disallows it) show; and whether it earns its place beside <, >, and s.
