### Why

The TUI never renews claims. With Worklease's default 15m TTL, an item started with `S` turns `expired` mid-session and any agent can take it with `bl claim`.

### Shape

- While the TUI runs and its session holds an active claim on an item in the snapshot, it renews at half the remaining lease, as `bl heartbeat -- COMMAND` does (design.md §452). The heartbeat runs in a command, never on the update loop (D14).
- A failed renewal shows a notice and retries on the next schedule while the lease is live; once it lapses, renewal stops and the row shows `expired`.
- Quitting stops renewal; the claim lapses after at most one TTL. Run-managed claims are never renewed.
- Single-project mode only, like the Claim column; workspaces show no claim overlay today.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] While the TUI holds an active claim, it renews at half the remaining lease, so a claim taken with S stays mine past its original TTL while the TUI stays open
- [ ] Renewal runs off the Bubble Tea update loop (D14) and never renews a run-managed or lapsed claim
- [ ] A failed renewal shows a notice and retries while the lease is live
- [ ] design.md and docs/tui.md describe TUI renewal and that quitting lets the claim lapse
